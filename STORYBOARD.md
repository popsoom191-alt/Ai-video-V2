# ลอยกระทง — Clawd's Krathong

Logline: Clawd wants to float a krathong under the full moon, but the wind blows its candle out, so a firefly comes and lights it again.

World: a river at night on Loy Krathong. Indigo sky, a big cream full moon, its reflection in the water, reeds on the near bank.
Colour arc: warm candle light → cold and dark once the candle goes out → warm again, with more lights on the river.

Motif: the small flame. It's lit, goes out, then comes back, and at the end there are many flames on the river.

Rhyme: the film opens on the moon and tilts down to Clawd. It ends by tilting up to the moon, which now shines over a river full of lights.

Clawd's arc: love → playful → surprised → sad → hopeful → starstruck → excited → love → happy.
